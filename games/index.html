Winter Solstice Writing Game

Write a short story, a carol, or just description or vibe of what would the Winter Solstice (Christmas) holiday look like if the Santa figure was the creature drawn as your prompt. Bonus points if they do wear the Santa hat.

Fight Scene Writing Game

Write a scene where two characters are in a fight. The prompt will give you some information for the reason of their fight. Their fight may be unbalanced, or one sided. Their weapons may be the same or differ. Consider if it would be appropriate for that weapon to be a weapon of convenience, or a hidden weapon.

Meet Cute Scene Writing Game

Write a scene where two characters meet for the first time and it leads to a romanticised meet that off page may lead to a relationship, i.e. the meet-cute part of the romantic comedy/romance.

Food Writing Game

Write a short description of somebody experiencing the following food for the first time. They have no experience of the food before and they come from a completely different culture to the origin of the food.

Perspective Writing Game

Think of a story you are familiar with, it can be a book, movie, TV show... Write of a summary of the events of that story from the limited perspective of a minor or background character. Keep it short, as if the character is telling the story to someone else. You can later make it into something longer. For example, Rosencrantz and Guildenstern are Dead is kind of a perspective of Hamlet from the point of view of the two characters. Another famous example would be a standup routine about Harry Potter from the perspective of a random Hogwarts student starting at the same time decrying the fact that some asshole bought all the sweets from the cart before they even got the chance to order some.
